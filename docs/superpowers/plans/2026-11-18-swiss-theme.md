# Architectural glass and Swiss theme

Apply the approved light glass direction using licensed architectural photography and add a separately selectable Swiss typographic theme.

- Store a stable appearance preference and share palette, type and shape choices across native screens and embedded management. Swiss uses warm paper, aligned type, square controls, visible rules and a small red selection marker. Preserve the active root and its navigation state during a change.
- Bundle a cropped architectural photograph for the glass toolbar and dock. Keep the photo at the edges of the workspace and document its attribution and license. Reduced transparency uses the flat surface.
- Verify theme defaults and contrast, settings selection and persistence, and WebView propagation. Build and publish same-signature APKs and record actual device coverage.
